# Other `shared/` modules and Game Variants

[English](shared-other.md) | [中文](shared-other_zh.md)

## `shared/draft/`

Pure functions for simultaneous selection (`initDraftState`, `processSubmit`, `tryAdvanceRound`, `finalizeDraft`); `createRoom` enables it with `draftMode: 'simultaneous'` and `draftPoolSize: 7..10`. With Farmers of the Moor, occupations, FoM minors and published minors draft in separate stages, the FoM minor pool derives from the implemented registry and refuses to start when short unless `allowIncompleteFarmersOfTheMoorMinorDeal` is set; Community Deck minors join only the published part.

## Solo opening hands

`shared/session/solo-hand-selection.ts` owns the solo pool quotas and complete-submission validation. Bootstrap filters and deduplicates before checking capacity. Random hands contain seven occupations and seven minors, or seven occupations plus three ordinary and four Moor minors. `soloHandMode: 'choose-7'` instead freezes ten occupations and ten ordinary minors, or ten occupations plus four ordinary and six Moor minors. Their keep quotas are seven/seven or seven/three/four. Short pools reject creation, including when multiplayer's incomplete-Moor option is set.

The independent `solo-hand-selection` phase and request expose every group together. `GameSession.submitSoloHandSelection` validates the owner and all groups before granting any hand. A single `soloHandSubmit` command uses the `solo-hand` input window and the ordinary durable receipt path. No command stores unconfirmed clicks. The authoritative candidates and confirmed `kept` history survive recovery; viewer projection hides them from other viewers. No sequential draft history is generated, and all candidates leave the ordinary draw decks. Parent candidates are frozen privately at creation and become available only after hands; direct parent dealing also settles after confirmation. First-round hooks then use the existing setup continuation.

Tests: `server/__tests__/standard-solo-hands-session.test.ts`, `server/connection/__tests__/solo-hand-handler.test.ts`, and `shared/session/__tests__/solo-hand-selection.test.ts`. Multiplayer draft remains covered by `server/game/__tests__/draft-session.test.ts`.

## Game Variants

A Game Variant changes setup, public state, Action Spaces, phase flow or scoring for one game; it is not a card source and adds no frontend rules.

- **Parent Cards** ([ADR 0005](../adr/0005-parent-cards-dedicated-expansion-runtime.md)) are structured data in `shared/parents/*` with assets pinned by `public-assets.ref`, separate from Card Source projections; selection, rewards and side quests are backend-authoritative and use non-public seeds.
- **Through the Seasons** keeps the four season spaces on the board and enables only the current one; its modifiers register in each Core's `ActionHookRegistry` and never write player `cardStates`. The season advances as soon as the new round number commits, before preparation; preparation then applies ordinary round growth before the season's accumulation adjustments (`applyRoundGrowth()`, then `applySeasonPreparationAdjustments()`), which clamp at zero. Seasonal gain and construction hooks carry their source through `actionContext` to the existing result fact ([ADR 0034](../adr/0034-seasonal-adjustments-belong-to-settlement-events.md)); `server/__tests__/seasonal-grants-session.test.ts` covers the pending, recovery and undo boundaries. Preparation captures ordinary growth and actual seasonal differences at settlement; affected zero additions remain public facts without creating extra resource movements.
- **Farmers of the Moor** ([ADR 0007](../adr/0007-farmers-of-the-moor-variant-runtime.md)) adds terrain, extensions, horses, heating and its major supply through the generic seams in [domain](domain.md). In multiplayer without simultaneous draft, `createInitialPlayers()` (`shared/session/state-bootstrap.ts`) deals each player 4 FoM minors plus 3 published minors; extra, custom and Community Deck minors join only the published deal, and a short FoM pool lowers every player's FoM portion equally only when `allowIncompleteFarmersOfTheMoorMinorDeal` is set. Solo opening hands use the strict quotas above.
- Actual Moor special actions share `buildMoorSpecialActionFlow` in `shared/moor/special-action-flow.ts`: normal and borrowed card actions, card grants, and scheduled offers validate their selected action and target before entering before reactions. Owner-grouped before sources use native activation and trigger-select; the body revalidates before committing. After base commit, a native completion frame freezes after sources and original receipts before required follow-ups. The shared engine completion boundary waits for payment, onBuy and animal handling, then joins terminal rule reactions by owner. Taking a card alone does not enter this lifecycle or emit placement/action-space use. See [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-unified-moor-special-action-lifecycle.md) and `server/__tests__/{moor-granted-lifecycle,moor-completion-window}-session.test.ts`.
- Card-action choices use authoritative card usability and validate the concrete action and target. Their presentation distinguishes the solo current card, market cards and borrowed face-up cards without changing ownership. Trusted card continuations retain their own timing and worker conditions; the ordinary public special-action entry keeps its work-phase and healthy-person requirements.
- **Snake Opening** reverses only round-1 work rotation ([Rotation, extra turns and supply people](session.md#rotation-extra-turns-and-supply-people)).
- With one actual participant, `shared/moor/solo-supply.ts` keeps the ten cards in one ordered supply: hidden draw, current or known discard. Preparation reveals or offers one keep/replace choice; exhaustion reuses the latest discard only at preparation. A real card use locks current through required follow-ups, then settles discard or the once-per-round two-food retention before the shared after window. The retention record clears after all round-end effects. Viewer projections expose current, discard and top-back action icons, never hidden identities or order; revealing a new face records a protected observation and undo boundary. All Moor games fix Major Improvement in round 1; solo includes Infirmary. Tests: `server/__tests__/standard-solo-moor-session.test.ts`.

## `shared/custom-code/`

Workshop cards are admitted only through the deployed Workshop Capability Contract ([ADR 0025](../adr/0025-workshop-capability-contract-is-fail-closed-and-opened-by-tests.md)).

- `ast-validator.ts` validates custom-card TypeScript with an AST: approved imports only, no forbidden APIs, network or I/O; `effect` must be an object literal; listener phases and effect keys must be in the contract allowlist.
- `sandbox-listener-actions.ts` is the single action list shared by prompts, the validator, server and browser manifests and doc sync (`pnpm run check:prompt-sync`); GitHub references cannot widen it.
- `flow-admission.ts`, `contract-admission.ts` and `listener-result-validator.ts` reject, before the engine, any output outside the contract: unlisted actions, foreign-card nodes and listeners, unopened node fields, wrong-typed opened fields and parameters, undocumented parameters, query results of the wrong type and writes to another card's or player's animal zones. Native card flows are not checked.
- Listener `costs` must come with matching `costAttribution`. Sandbox constraints are documented in [`docs/CUSTOM_CARD_SANDBOX.md`](../CUSTOM_CARD_SANDBOX.md); runtime isolation lives in `server/custom-code/` ([server/custom-code/: Isolated execution](server.md#servercustom-code-isolated-execution)).

Tests: `shared/custom-code/__tests__/`, `server/__tests__/workshop-contract-*-session.test.ts`.

## `shared/i18n/` and `shared/utils/`

`shared/i18n/` owns the synchronous translation/catalog API and package validation; `PromptKey` lives in `shared/contract/prompt-keys.ts`. Authored translations and the BGA importer belong to [open-agricola-i18n](https://github.com/titanxxh/open-agricola-i18n), with one source JSON and one complete runtime JSON per language. `i18n.ref` pins its commit and manifest checksum. `scripts/prepare-language-packs.ts` verifies package/source checksums and stages ignored build caches; `AGRICOLA_I18N_DIR` supplies a local checkout with the same pinned content.

`client/services/language-pack.ts` discovers languages from the manifest, downloads only the selected complete package and verifies its identity/hash before registration. Locale switches commit only after loading succeeds. Official card faces and parent texts use the same catalog as UI, prompts and logs; authored custom-card fields are normalized per supplied preview/room/replay card and never modify the global catalog. English completion and legacy-key normalization happen in the language repository at build time. Workshop PR generation strips embedded translations from submitted Card Sources and supplies a separate initial language patch in the proposal; draft updates require a fresh patch and a matching package version before merge. Tests: `scripts/__tests__/language-pack-boundary.test.ts`, `client/services/__tests__/language-pack.test.ts`, `e2e-tests/card-localization.spec.ts`.

`shared/utils/` holds generic helpers with no business rules; `rng.ts` owns the Explicit Seed generator and HMAC-SHA256 wide-seed streams.
