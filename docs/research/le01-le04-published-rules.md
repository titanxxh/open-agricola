## Confirmed setup and publication facts

Lookout identifies LE01–LE04 as four event cards included among the anniversary edition's L-Deck material. Its FAQ permits selecting one randomly at game start, exposing it to everyone, and applying it to every player. This is optional setup. The source does not require an event in every game or authorize combining several events. The visible card footer also describes choosing and exposing one event at the beginning. [English publisher FAQ and image](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/en/games/15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html)

The box is advertised for 1–4 players. Separate FAQ answers permit combining the anniversary material with the Revised Edition Farmers of the Moor expansion and 5–6-player expansion. Those are general compatibility statements, not detailed LE interaction rulings. They do not justify a blanket prohibition on those combinations. [English publisher FAQ](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/en/games/15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html)

Neither retrieved FAQ specifies the order relative to drafting, solo hand selection, or Parent Cards. It also does not define rematches, a digital event selector, undo, or restoration. Those remain project decisions. [English FAQ](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/en/games/15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html), [German FAQ](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/de/games/15jahreagricolajubilaeumsbox.html)

## Base-rule context does not settle every combination

The rulebook distinguishes an action space's occupation cost from an additional cost printed in an occupation's text. The occupation section near the end of the rulebook explains both. Clearing the final combined payment would erase that published distinction. Whether a card-granted fixed payment represents tuition, an individual occupation cost, or another effect still requires examining that grant. [English rulebook](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/upload/en_15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html_Argicola_Aniversery_Rules_155_EN_WEB.pdf)

The solo rules on printed page 12 require three food for an adult and one for a newborn. The ordinary rules allow one pet in the house. These are the base quantities the event may modify. Neither statement establishes how LE discounts combine with an extra feeding obligation or another animal-capacity effect. [English rulebook](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/upload/en_15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html_Argicola_Aniversery_Rules_155_EN_WEB.pdf)

The retrieved appendix describes Through the Seasons and other included variants. Searches for the LE identifiers and English event names found no LE-specific clarification there. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no other official ruling exists. The FAQ's general compatibility statements do not specify discount ordering, species grouping, or an event's interaction with heating. [English appendix](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/upload/de_15jahreagricolajubilaeumsbox.html_Argicola_Aniversery_Appendix_155_EN_WEB.pdf), [English FAQ](https://www.lookout-spiele.de/en/games/15yearsofagricolaanniversarybox.html)

## Questions requiring further evidence or project decisions

No event-specific ruling for the following cases was found in the retrieved publisher material. An omission is not a prohibition.

| Area | Precise unresolved question |
| --- | --- |
| Complete adopted text | Can an unobstructed original scan confirm every qualifier hidden in LE01, LE02, and LE04? The visible core effect is established, but the full sentences are not. |
| Pet Lovers | How does the house allowance combine with Animal Tamer or other room-capacity effects? Is the different-species condition confined to animals housed through LE01? Does a horse count as an eligible pet with Farmers of the Moor? How are physical room positions assigned when the allowance exceeds the room count? |
| Good Harvest | Is the one-food reduction applied once to the family's total, including in solo play? How does it combine with individual feeding modifiers, extra food obligations, nonstandard feeding phases, and a zero-food requirement? Does it leave heating entirely separate? |
| Wandering Carpenters | How does the mandatory wood reduction combine with a resource substitution, a fixed-price replacement, an already-free play, or a card-granted improvement opportunity? The printed general scope does not supply an algorithm for these combinations. |
| Inquisitive Minds | Which card-granted payments are occupation tuition? Which include another fee or an individual printed cost? The published exception must remain distinct, but an internal `exactCost` field cannot determine the rule by itself. |
| Opening | Must players know the event before choosing or drafting hands and Parent Cards? When is the server's choice committed? Which creation modes need random selection or an explicit selector? |
| Lifecycle | Does a same-configuration rematch make a fresh event choice? What must recovery and undo preserve after public disclosure? The published physical rules do not describe these digital operations. |
| Combinations | How should LE interact with Parent Cards and the supported Through the Seasons rules? General anniversary-box compatibility does not answer their individual timing and quantity cases. |

These questions belong in [the rules-and-combinations decision](https://github.com/titanxxh/open-agricola/issues/1493) and [the opening-and-rematch decision](https://github.com/titanxxh/open-agricola/issues/1494). An adopted answer without a unique published basis must be labelled Project Rule Interpretation. This research does not select those answers.

## Handoff

The investigation establishes the target edition, four identifiers, readable core effects, optional shared event setup, and general Revised Edition compatibility. It also records where the primary evidence is incomplete. Subsequent decision sessions can use this report without treating the original feature issue, a software implementation, a community summary, or a different Agricola edition as an official ruling.
