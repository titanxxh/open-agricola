# 48. Cook reserve animals during acquisition reorganization

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-19

A player took two sheep and wanted to house one and cook the other. The Exchange Center exposed the recipe, and the animal reorganization panel offered placement and discard. Ordinary animal payment can also consume housed animals before using the reserve. The reorganization panel needs a way to cook the surplus that the player sees in the draft. [Issue #1842](https://github.com/titanxxh/open-agricola/issues/1842)

## Decision

- Show **Convert to food** beside the unassigned counts and in the discard confirmation when at least one recipe is legal. This applies to reorganization caused by acquiring animals, including action spaces and card effects. The existing Anytime Window and breeding rules govern eligibility, including for births outside harvest. Players can still confirm discard explicitly. [Breeding settlement](0034-seasonal-adjustments-belong-to-settlement-events.md)
- For each species, cap payment at the lesser of the unassigned draft count and the authoritative Pending Reserve Animal count. Use the player's edited draft, including animals they intentionally leave unassigned. The acquisition marks when the opportunity opens. Payment draws from the current reserve by species and preserves already housed animals.
- Reuse the Exchange Center's recipe and quantity controls. Offer recipes currently available as an anytime exchange that pay only animals from the reserve and directly yield only food. Check each source's eligibility and limits. The player chooses recipes and counts. Recipes in the same batch share a per-species payment limit. The standard Exchange Center remains available for other payments, including recipes that require placed animals.
- Opening the panel, editing quantities, and cancelling are local operations. One explicit confirmation submits the chosen recipes and draft for validation in the current reorganization window. The server checks the acting seat, pending request, room version, offered and live recipes, draft and total reserve payment before committing. Settle through the existing Exchange Batch and Anytime Activation paths so ordinary events and card reactions run. Retry uses the existing command receipt.
- After settlement and its reactions, return to the same reorganization when it remains active. Keep valid draft assignments and show affected animals as unassigned. The player confirms placement or discard separately. Undoing the exchange restores the animals, food, and pending reorganization. A reconnect in the same tab keeps the valid draft for the same pending request and refreshes offers. A full reload rebuilds the draft from authoritative state, including any committed exchange and unfinished reactions.
- Persist the acquisition origin with the pending reorganization. Earlier pending states without that origin resume through their existing placement, discard and standard exchange paths. Future acquisition reorganizations in those games can offer reserve cooking.

## Acceptance

- In a new two-player work-phase session, give the acting player a Fireplace, no pasture, an empty house pet slot and two sheep on Sheep Market. After collection, offer one sheep for two food. Confirm the exchange, then confirm placement. Verify one sheep remains as a pet, the player gained two food, the exchange is logged once, and no sheep was discarded. Repeat with a sheep already housed and verify that its confirmed placement survives the exchange.
- Verify card-granted animals also qualify. Include a swap that gives the player an unassigned animal of a different species while the total number of animals stays the same. Check capacity-only reorganization with displaced animals and a Fireplace, manual reorganization, ordinary harvest breeding, and unplaced card or season newborns. These cases use their existing options. Check that placed-only recipes stay out of the reserve offer, and that a batch that exceeds the shared reserve limit is rejected in full.
- In the browser, edit an assignment, open and cancel the conversion, and verify that the edited draft returns. Reopen, make a partial conversion, verify the remaining count and valid assignments, and then confirm placement. Check that the recipe choice and confirmation are reachable on a narrow viewport.
- Exercise duplicate, wrong-seat, and stale submissions, undo, and recovery with an acquisition reorganization pending. Include a retained state without the origin. After cooking a reserve boar with Master Tanner in play, recover during its follow-up choice. Verify that the choice completes before reorganization resumes for the original actor and that the exchange is recorded once.
